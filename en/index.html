Why me

What you actually get.

01

The story, not just the photo

I am not after perfectly arranged smiles. I am after the moments in between — the emotion, the atmosphere, and the details you never notice on the day but will remember for years.

02

Fast delivery

No waiting for months. You get a complete, edited gallery that is easy to download or pass on to family, shortly after the event.

03

Real event experience

Over 70 events — birthdays, weddings, christenings and concerts. I know where to stand, when to press the shutter, and how to stay out of your moment.

My journey

How I got here.

2024

The first shutter

I picked up photography as a hobby — learning light, composition, and the patience to wait for the right frame instead of forcing it.

January 2026

The first event

A friend asked me to shoot my first event. That was the moment the hobby became something else — I have not stopped since.

Today

Almost every weekend

Birthdays, weddings, christenings, concerts. Every event has sharpened my style and technique, and now I deliver a complete gallery, fast, that tells the story of your day.
